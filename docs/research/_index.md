# research/

Primary-source investigations: what the docs, specs, and source code of other projects say, with every claim cited and unconfirmed claims marked **(inferred)** or **(anecdotal)**. Each note answers one question behind a bug record or decision.

- [terminal-paste-fidelity.md](terminal-paste-fidelity.md) — why typed text loses fidelity in terminals while a real paste does not: Claude Code's 800-character paste collapse, xterm.js and Chromium handling of synthetic Unicode key events, bracketed paste, and the mitigations River could take. Behind [../planning/0031_terminal-insert-fidelity.md](../planning/0031_terminal-insert-fidelity.md)
- [shortcut-registration.md](shortcut-registration.md) — how River should register recorded shortcuts: Carbon `RegisterEventHotKey` (no TCC, press/release, the macOS 15.0–15.1 ⌥-only refusal, silent conflicts, no left/right, main-thread delivery) against a `keyDown` event tap and today's listen-only `flagsChanged` tap, with on-machine probes (key delivery confirmed by hand) and a hybrid recommendation. Behind [../planning/0036_settings-and-shortcuts.md](../planning/0036_settings-and-shortcuts.md)
- [shortcut-recorder.md](shortcut-recorder.md) — how the settings panel should capture a shortcut: local `NSEvent` monitor in a key panel, side-specific modifier bits, lone-modifier vs combination capture, suspending River's own hot keys, `CopySymbolicHotKeys` warnings, Caps Lock and Fn. Behind [../planning/0036_settings-and-shortcuts.md](../planning/0036_settings-and-shortcuts.md)
