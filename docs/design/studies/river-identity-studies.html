Microphone, traced from the reference. Every dimension is measured from the photo's pixels rather than drawn by eye: the head's proportions and corner radius, six top slots aligned along their lower ends with the outer pair giving way to the corners, eight middle rows split by the central rib and stepping back where the emblem sat, and six bottom slots, twice the height of the top ones, aligned along their upper ends. The emblem itself is left as empty space. Regularized to be symmetric and scored against the photo's grooves at a mean overlap of 92%. Every groove is one rounded stroke, and the silhouette is always present at low ink. Level wakes the three groups from the bottom up, each stroke on a short stagger from the center outward.

Thresholds: the bottom slots wake above 0.12, the middle rows above 0.42, the top slots above 0.72. Waking is only a change of ink: a groove fades from 25% to full over about a tenth of a second and back over half a second, with the center grooves of each group leading by a few frames. Uniform ink, no accent. The shape never changes size between the empty and the filled state. Transcribing holds every group awake and breathes the whole head slowly.

The icon is open again. The drawn letters below did not earn their place and are kept only as the record of what was tried; the next attempt starts from a different premise.

A lowercase serif r, drawn as calligraphic strokes. The stem is the main channel; the shoulder leaves it low and climbs away as a tributary, and the join between them is where the letter carries its feeling. Each stroke is built from a centerline and a width profile, so the tapers are continuous rather than drawn point by point. Every r is measured after drawing and centered in the squircle by its ink box, at every size and on the inverted tile.

The references are real faces, shown only for the shape of their lowercase r. The drawn letters borrow stem weight and shoulder height from them and depart in the join and the terminal. Centering: the icon measures its own bounding box and offsets it to the squircle's center, then nudges 1.5% left so the leg's visual weight reads balanced. The same offset applies at 16 px.

Goal for this indicator, checked against the held frames and the live panel:

  1. At rest, three clearly separated lines, almost straight, still drifting slowly. Never a ruler, never a tangle.
  2. Rising level widens the water smoothly, with round two's timing. No jump, no snap.
  3. At peak, full meander; strands may cross but never weave symmetrically.
  4. Both ends fade to nothing. Nothing clips.
  5. Transcribing holds a small steady motion, distinct from both rest and speech.
