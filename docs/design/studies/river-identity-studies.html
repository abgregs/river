Every stage, held, at 2×

Ready is the menu bar glyph at 48 pt: middle slat at full ink, the others at 55%, stroke 1.1 units. Listening changes ink only: a slat rises from 55% to full ink over about 0.15 s and settles back over 0.6 s, its stroke going from 1.1 to 1.3 units so the gaps between slats stay open; the middle slat leads above level 0.06, the inner pair above 0.18, the outer pair above 0.55. Transcribing is the dotted glyph: each slat becomes a row of dots whose first and last sit exactly on the slat's endpoints, so the outline is the same as Ready and Listening; a soft crest of full ink travels across the dots every 2.4 s over a 55% base, and no dot ever moves, appears, or disappears. Solid and dotted crossfade over about 0.2 s rather than cutting. The bar itself fades in and out over 0.22 s with a 6 pt rise, ease-out both ways. Under Reduce Motion the dots hold at full ink and the crossfade and fade become instant. Nothing changes size. The 16 px cell beside the Ready frame is the menu bar glyph from the same function. Motion has only been judged in stills by the agent.

River, now a strong candidate. Lines only, in a snug capsule with even padding; the word and the timer are gone. Uniform ink on all three strands. Ink follows the level through a smoothstep over the first half of the range, so on release it settles in step with the motion.

Transcribing makes the strands beaded, the same solid-to-dots change the menu bar glyph makes: beads sit every 4.5 px along each strand and ride its wave at the calm transcribing motion. Lead: the beads stay put and a crest of ink travels along them every 2.4 s, the same crest as the microphone indicator's dots. Alternate: the beads themselves drift downstream at 14 px per second, entering and leaving under the end fade so none ever pops. Strokes and beads crossfade over 0.2 s. Under Reduce Motion the beads hold at full ink and do not drift.

Goal for this indicator, checked against the held frames and the live panel:

  1. At rest, three living strands: the 0.5 shape slightly compressed, drifting at under half speed, at 44% ink breathing slowly. Never flat, never sub-pixel.
  2. Rising level brightens and widens the water smoothly, with round two's timing; on release the ink dims in step with the motion settling to its floor. No jump, no snap.
  3. At peak, full meander; strands may cross but never weave symmetrically.
  4. Both ends fade to nothing inside the capsule. Nothing clips, and the bar never changes size.
  5. Transcribing beads the strands, the river's version of the slats becoming dots, with a crest of ink along them: unmistakably a different state.

Turquoise variants, more saturated and cyan-leaning

Nine candidates built in OKLCH so each keeps one hue and only chroma and lightness change. Each has a light-desktop value, the most vivid value at that hue that still carries text at 4.5:1 on white, and a dark-desktop value for charcoal. The light values converge: sRGB cannot hold a cyan that is both vivid and dark enough for text on white, so every text-safe light value lands near chroma 0.095. The variants really differ on charcoal, where they run from the current 5:1 to a 10:1 neon. On the vivid dark values a pressed control needs charcoal text, not white.
