Every stage, held, at 2×

Ready is the menu bar glyph at 48 pt: middle slat at full ink, the others at 55%, stroke 1.1 units. Listening changes ink only: a slat rises from 55% to full ink over about 0.15 s and settles back over 0.6 s, its stroke going from 1.1 to 1.3 units so the gaps between slats stay open; the middle slat leads above level 0.06, the inner pair above 0.18, the outer pair above 0.55. Transcribing is the dotted glyph with the dots drifting right one pitch every 3.5 seconds; under Reduce Motion they hold still. Nothing changes size. The 16 px cell beside the Ready frame is the menu bar glyph from the same function. Motion has only been judged in stills by the agent.

Instrument Serif, changed in one place, visibly. The lowercase r is taken directly from Instrument Serif, point for point (Young Serif was dropped as not legible). One region is then changed: the inner join where the underside of the shoulder meets the stem. In the font the shoulder leaves the stem as a hairline and the crotch is empty; here the underside bows outward and sweeps down into the stem, filling the crotch and arriving tangent to it a third of the way down, so the shoulder reads as a tributary merging into the main channel. The overlay below draws the shipped outline over the modified letter so the change is plain. Nothing else moves: the face's stem, serifs, and ball terminal are untouched. The letter is centered in the squircle by its ink box, both axes, at every size.

River, parked. Uniform ink on all three strands. Motion above the rest state is exactly round two's: the same drift, easing, and amplitude curve, which read as fluid at 0.5 and 1.0. The rest state is new: the amplitude is exactly zero, so the strands are three perfectly flat lines centered on pixel rows, dimmed to 40% ink with a slow breath. Round eight's rest state looked grainy because its sub-pixel wobble on hairlines was split between pixel rows by antialiasing and then drifted; flat lines on pixel rows cannot do that. Rising level blends width, ink, and amplitude into the motion with round two's timing.

Goal for this indicator, checked against the held frames and the live panel:

  1. At rest, three perfectly flat lines on pixel rows at 40% ink, breathing slowly. No drift, no wobble, nothing sub-pixel.
  2. Rising level brightens and widens the water smoothly, with round two's timing. No jump, no snap.
  3. At peak, full meander; strands may cross but never weave symmetrically.
  4. Both ends fade to nothing. Nothing clips.
  5. Transcribing holds a small steady motion, distinct from both rest and speech.

Menu bar

Redrawn from the reference: a single river seen from above, wide at the near bank, winding in one S and tapering to a point on the horizon at the upper right. Ready is the outline; Listening fills the water; Transcribing keeps the fill and dots the banks.

Menu bar · the r, three attempts

Built the way the icon was: the stem and shoulder centerlines are derived from the Young Serif r's outline (the average of the shoulder's outer and inner edges, the stem's midline), mapped into the 16 px box, and loosely modified so the shoulder leaves the stem as a confluence. The lines share the slat glyph's stroke and states: 1.1 units at Ready with the secondary lines at 55%, 1.3 and full ink at Listening, dotted at Transcribing. The aim is a family resemblance between this mark and the three-line indicator: the same thin curved lines, at rest and awake.

Monoline r

Two lines: a straight stem and the shoulder joining it tangentially, as a tributary. The shoulder is the secondary line at Ready.

Three lines

The same shoulder over a stem with a faint river bend, plus the foot serif as a third short line. Three lines, like the indicator.

Filled r, River join

The icon's own letter at 16 px, filled at 70% when Ready and full when Listening; Transcribing dots its outline. Not taken: the dots collapse at 16 px. The two line-based attempts stay as candidates.

Accepted as is. Five slats, shortened toward the top and bottom, with distinct Ready, Listening, and Transcribing states. Parked as the working menu bar glyph; shown here unchanged for reference beside the river glyph.
