Where a menu bar glyph sits beside a frame, it is the same drawing at 16 px, produced by the same function, so the match is by construction. Motion: a slat's ink rises from 55% to full in about a tenth of a second and falls back over half a second; the stroke thickens from 1.2 to 1.6 units with it, as the menu bar does between Ready and Listening. Thresholds: middle slat above 0.06, inner pair above 0.18, outer pair above 0.55. Transcribing drifts the dots one pitch every three seconds; under Reduce Motion the dots hold still. The motion has only been judged in stills by the agent; it needs your eye on the live panel.

From a real outline, changed in one place. The lowercase r is taken directly from an openly licensed typeface, point for point. One thing is then changed: the inner join where the underside of the shoulder meets the stem. In the font it is a short notch; here it becomes a long concave sweep that arrives tangent to the stem well below the shoulder, so the shoulder reads as a tributary merging into the main channel. Nothing else moves, so the letter keeps the face's stem, serifs, and terminal. The letter is centered in the squircle by its ink box, both axes, at every size.

Goal for this indicator, checked against the held frames and the live panel:

  1. At rest, three clearly separated lines, almost straight, still drifting slowly. Never a ruler, never a tangle.
  2. Rising level widens the water smoothly, with round two's timing. No jump, no snap.
  3. At peak, full meander; strands may cross but never weave symmetrically.
  4. Both ends fade to nothing. Nothing clips.
  5. Transcribing holds a small steady motion, distinct from both rest and speech.
