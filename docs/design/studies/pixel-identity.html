River — Drop & Play

The chosen direction. In the panel the mark is live. It is the r at rest, drops into a 4×4 meter while you speak, and returns to the r while an ink crest runs the stroke. In the menu bar it is static: one template icon per state, swapped instantly, and tinted by the system for light and dark menu bars. The native build is planning 0029; the rounds that led here are recorded in pixel-identity.md. Serve this folder over localhost (not file://) to use the microphone.

Panel

Rest: the r. Listening: all sixteen pixels drop together into a 4×4 meter in 260 ms. Each column is its own readout, rising fast and falling slowly, out of step with the others. Silence settles all four to the lit bottom row. Transcribing: the pixels return to the r, and an ink crest traces the stroke from its foot.

Panel mock at 2×: the mark alone, as shipped, with 12 pt padding.

Menu bar

Static template icons, one per state, swapped instantly when the state changes, so nothing moves in the menu bar. Each maps to what the panel is doing. They are drawn denser than the panel, with 2.5 pt cells and 0.5 pt gaps in a 17.5 pt square, because at this size wider gaps turn the mark into dots.
