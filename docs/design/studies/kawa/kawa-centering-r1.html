What this round decides

Decided 29 October 2026: halfway, on both axes

The mark is centred on the point halfway between its box centre and its ink centroid, on both axes, in every frame: the panel, the menu bar image and the app icon. Against box centring it sits 0.89 pt left and 0.87 pt down in the panel, 0.47 pt left and 0.47 pt down in the menu bar. The offset is measured once, from 3C at rest, and stored as a constant in design units, so it scales with the mark and never moves with a state. A future drawing with its own weight, such as a menu bar drawing, gets its own measurement.

The questions as they were put, for the record:

  1. Horizontal. Which centre reads as centred in the panel: the box, the middle stroke, halfway, or the ink centroid? Suggested: the middle stroke. It is the axis people see in 川, it lands within 0.2 pt of halfway, and it stays put when a stroke's weight changes, where the ink centroid moves.
  2. Vertical. Box centring leaves the weight 1.7 pt above the panel's centre, because the release and the needle stretch the box downward with little ink. Halfway lowers the mark 0.87 pt; the ink centroid lowers it 1.74 pt. Suggested: halfway, which keeps the weight just above centre, where the eye expects it.
  3. One rule for both surfaces. The menu bar shifts are about half the panel's in points. One rule keeps the two surfaces matched by construction; a separate menu bar rule would only make sense with its own drawing.

Not in this round

  • A menu bar drawing. At 16 pt the panel's strokes are about 1.3 pt wide. The menu bar may want its own weight, and its stems snapped to pixels, which would move them by under half a point.
  • The mark's height in the menu bar. 16 pt here, the height of today's r.
  • Motion and colour. Muted strokes on appear, filled when audio is heard, and size as a signal come after the mark's place is set.
