# design/

Identity and interface direction for River — what the app should look, move, and sound like, and the principles each surface is designed against. Behavior lives in [requirements/](../requirements/_index.md); structure lives in [architecture/](../architecture/_index.md); this folder is about feel.

- [direction.md](direction.md) — the identity brief: the "text flows in and follows you" idea, five principles (motion is state; never steal focus; fluid not bouncy; platform first; quiet by default), a per-surface direction (icon, menu bar glyphs, HUD, sound cues, onboarding, page, DMG), constraints, open decisions, and the sequence for the design phase
- [identity-studies.md](identity-studies.md) — the locked state of the identity studies (2026-09-16): confirmed palette and rules, the accepted microphone menu bar glyph, open indicator and icon candidates, every rejected attempt with its reason, and the working rules the rounds taught. Read before any icon, glyph, indicator, or palette work
- [pixel-identity.md](pixel-identity.md) — round ten (2026-09-24), superseded on every surface by the stones (2026-10-14): the pixel-grid r that replaced the slat glyph, the river indicator, and the slat icon; what was approved, the earlier rules it deliberately revises, every rejected attempt, and the working rules it taught. Read with identity-studies.md
- [kawa-identity.md](kawa-identity.md) — the current mark (2026-11-02): 川 (variant 3C), centred halfway between its box and its ink; the menu bar glyphs (the red recording dot, muted while transcribing) and the app icon; the panel's choreography and its one treatment, Glass, with its tuning from the Glass study and reasons (Stream, a second style, dropped 2026-11-03); the level scale calibrated on device; preparing, Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency; monochrome; what is deferred and open; and the study record in `studies/kawa/`. Read before any mark, glyph, or indicator work
- [stones-identity.md](stones-identity.md) — the mark from 2026-10-14, superseded by the kawa identity (2026-11-02; the menu bar's bars 2026-11-04): three stones inspired by a bold lowercase r that morph into level bars; the motion (350 ms hold, 40 ms stagger, still bars in silence, no travelling-light effects), the accent meaning "River is on", the panel interaction spike
- [color.md](color.md) — the color roles: charcoal and ink carry everything, and the one turquoise accent means "on" (a control you switched on; since 2026-11-02 the panel is monochrome); where it never appears; the opaque lit HUD surface (2026-11-04); the menu bar's recording red; measured contrast, and guidance for future surfaces. Read before adding color anywhere
- [studies/](studies/) — the interactive study pages (rounds one to nine, `pixel-identity.html` for round ten, `stones-motion.html` with its generator `stones-mark.py` for the stones identity, and `kawa/` for the kawa identity), the microphone groove geometry measured from the reference photo, and the script that reproduces the measurement
