# design/

Identity and interface direction for River — what the app should look, move, and sound like, and the principles each surface is designed against. Behavior lives in [requirements/](../requirements/_index.md); structure lives in [architecture/](../architecture/_index.md); this folder is about feel.

- [direction.md](direction.md) — the identity brief: the "text flows in and follows you" idea, five principles (motion is state; never steal focus; fluid not bouncy; platform first; quiet by default), a per-surface direction (icon, menu bar glyphs, HUD, sound cues, onboarding, page, DMG), constraints, open decisions, and the sequence for the design phase
- [identity-studies.md](identity-studies.md) — the locked state of the identity studies (2026-09-16): confirmed palette and rules, the accepted microphone menu bar glyph, open indicator and icon candidates, every rejected attempt with its reason, and the working rules the rounds taught. Read before any icon, glyph, indicator, or palette work
- [pixel-identity.md](pixel-identity.md) — round ten (2026-09-24), superseded on every surface by the stones (2026-10-14): the pixel-grid r that replaced the slat glyph, the river indicator, and the slat icon; what was approved, the earlier rules it deliberately revises, every rejected attempt, and the working rules it taught. Read with identity-studies.md
- [stones-identity.md](stones-identity.md) — the current mark (2026-10-14): three stones inspired by a bold lowercase r that morph into level bars; the motion (350 ms hold, 40 ms stagger, still bars in silence, no travelling-light effects), the accent meaning "River is on", the panel interaction spike, and what is open. Read before any mark, glyph, or indicator work
- [color.md](color.md) — the color roles: charcoal and ink carry everything, and the one turquoise accent means "on" (River recording or transcribing, or a control you switched on); where it never appears, status colors, measured contrast, and guidance for future surfaces. Read before adding color anywhere
- [studies/](studies/) — the interactive study pages (rounds one to nine, `pixel-identity.html` for round ten, and `stones-motion.html` with its generator `stones-mark.py` for the stones identity), the microphone groove geometry measured from the reference photo, and the script that reproduces the measurement
