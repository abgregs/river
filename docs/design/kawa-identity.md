# Design: The kawa identity

River's mark since 2026-11-02: 川 (kawa, "river"), three brush strokes, in monochrome; the one color beside it is the menu bar's recording dot. It replaces the stones of [stones-identity.md](stones-identity.md) in the floating panel, the menu bar, and the app icon. In the panel, River shows your voice with one treatment, **Glass**. The shape was chosen on 2026-10-29 and Glass and its tuning approved on 2026-11-02, both by the maintainer; a second style, Stream, was dropped on 2026-11-03, Glass was retuned and the floating panels given an opaque lit surface on the Glass study (2026-11-03 and 2026-11-04), and the menu bar's recording dot was chosen on 2026-11-04 (below). The build is [../planning/0035_kawa-identity-and-indicator-styles.md](../planning/0035_kawa-identity-and-indicator-styles.md); the study record is [studies/kawa/](studies/kawa/) (below).

## The mark

- **Shape: variant 3C.** Three strokes, drawn by a brush model (an elliptical nib stamped along a spine, its pressure, aspect and angle keyed along the way) and traced as cubic Béziers: a left stroke that swoops down to the left, a short middle stroke with a broad head tapering to a blunt finish, and a tall right stroke whose lower half slims into a needle point on its left edge. Clean ink silhouettes, no dry-brush texture. Chosen from three shape rounds (maintainer, 2026-10-29).
- **Centring: halfway.** Every frame places the point halfway between the ink's box centre and its centroid, on both axes, at its own centre. **Why:** centring the box put the tall right strokes visibly right of centre, because the left stroke's swoop widens the box; centring the ink overcorrected (maintainer, 2026-10-29). The point is measured once by the centring study and stored as a constant, `KawaMark.centre` (517.8, 474.8 in the 1000-unit design square), not recomputed per frame.
- **Sizes.** 30 pt tall in the 48 pt panel (`indicatorMarkHeight`); 16 pt tall in the 18 pt menu bar image, 14 pt beside the recording dot; 0.56 of the squircle's height in the app icon (`appIconMarkFraction`).
- **Code.** `KawaMark` holds the strokes, their spines, the box and the centring point, and places the mark (`transform(height:centredAt:)`). Its data, `KawaMark+Outlines.swift`, is generated by `kawa-swift.mjs` from the study's data and never edited by hand. The panel, the glyph and the icon all draw these shapes, so they match by construction.

## Menu bar and app icon

- **Ready** is the solid 川, a template image the system paints white or black like every other icon there. The menu bar has no motion (maintainer, 2026-10-29).
- **Listening** is 川, 14 pt and left of centre, with a **recording dot** at the top right in akaneiro (茜色), a muted madder red: the convention for a device that is recording, and the one color in the menu bar (maintainer, 2026-11-04). **Transcribing** keeps the dot, in the bar's own white or black at a reduced strength (55 %): related to recording without being on, as River works on what it heard. Red means only that River is recording a dictation (`.recording`). In ready mode the microphone stays open while River is idle ([../planning/0033_warm-microphone-pre-roll.md](../planning/0033_warm-microphone-pre-roll.md)), and macOS's own microphone indicator shows that; River's red marks the dictation. A template image cannot carry the red, so River draws both dots over a template 川 when the bar draws them. The dot changes state at once: a fade was tried on 2026-11-04, and the menu bar does not redraw for it. Every state is the same 18 pt, so nothing shifts between states (maintainer, 2026-11-04). The exceptional states keep their SF Symbols ([../architecture/app-state-and-menu-bar.md](../architecture/app-state-and-menu-bar.md)); the red's values are in [color.md](color.md).
- **The app icon** is 川 in `ink` on the charcoal squircle, centred halfway.

The menu bar PNGs and the icon are re-rendered from `KawaMark` by tests ([../conventions/test-harnesses.md](../conventions/test-harnesses.md)); the dots are drawn by River at runtime.

## The panel: choreography

The panel runs one sequence, keyed to four events: the press, the first level reading (audio flowing), the release, and the text typed. The timeline (`KawaIndicatorTimeline`) is pure and unit-tested.

| Moment | What the panel does |
|---|---|
| Press | The panel fades in over 0.22 s on a strong ease-out, rising 6 pt. From 0.08 s after the press, the muted strokes fade in over **0.45 s** on a sine ease-in-out, to **20 %** |
| Audio flowing | The Glass layer starts **0.20 s** before the strokes are fully in, or at the first level reading if that is later. A dead microphone never starts it: the muted strokes stay alone |
| Release | The layer leaves from 0.05 s after the release, over 0.4 s |
| Transcribing | The muted strokes hold until the text is typed |
| Typed | The strokes fade over 0.45 s, starting 0.20 s before the layer is gone (or when the text is typed, if later); the panel fades over 0.3 s on a softer ease-out, ending 0.08 s after the strokes |

**Why:** the entry is a sequence, panel then strokes then the voice, and the overlap lets each flow into the next; a 0.2 s strong ease-out popped the strokes in, and the sine fade fixed it ("the entrance is phenomenal", maintainer, 2026-10-31). The exit mirrors the entry. The longest exit after River goes idle is 0.78 s, a cancel's, and the window stays up that long (`RecordingIndicatorCoordinator.orderOutDelay`). A new press during the exit takes the panel over at the opacity it had reached, so it never flashes.

**Rules the study taught:**

- **Motion means sound.** Glass is still in silence: a live microphone that hears nothing shows the frost, unmoving, so it reads as quiet, not dead. The layer waits for `AppState.isAudioFlowing`, so a dead microphone shows the muted strokes only.
- **The strokes stay fixed.** The mark never morphs; the voice is a layer over or through it. A morph concept and a level-in-the-strokes concept were studied and dropped (2026-10-31 and 2026-11-02).
- **Ripples are allowed again.** The stones' ban on ripples and travelling light was lifted for this identity (maintainer, 2026-10-29); Glass's ripples are the voice. A glow and a shimmer while transcribing were dropped (2026-10-30).

## Glass

A pane of frost over the strokes that your voice ripples and clears. While the level is above the threshold, drops fall on the strokes at a steady **2 per second**, each in one of three tiers (soft, normal, raised) picked by the loudest level since the previous drop. Each drop sends out a ring and clears a window in the frost around it. The look below was retuned on the Glass study (maintainer, 2026-11-03; the page stays local, its chosen values are in `Constants`), so a normal voice plainly shows, a raised one more, and the frosted and cleared states read apart; the tiers and the threshold are the approved ones.

- **Ripples:** size **1.6** (width, wavelength and life; a ripple lasts about 2.8 s), on a stroke's spine away from its ends, nudged up to 0.6 pt off it.
- **Threshold:** sensitivity 0.6, a level of 0.32 − 0.26 × 0.6 = **0.164**.
- **Tiers:** normal from a peak of 0.32, loud from 0.60. Per tier, soft to raised, the ring's strength (its light and its bend) is **0.6**, **1.2** and **1.4**, and its window's radius **12**, **20** and **28** pt: small for soft, about the mark's width for normal, most of the panel for raised. A window holds open **1.2 s**, then fades back to frost over **0.6 s**.
- **Shader:** refraction **0.4**, highlights **0.8** (the study's 0–1 sliders, which the shader scales by 1.6 and 1.4). The light comes from the panel's own lit corner (`hudLightAngle`, 147°), so the panel and the ripples share one light. Frost **0.72**, a blur that grows with it plus a grained veil, the tint moved 16 % toward the text color in both appearances ([color.md](color.md)); the windows' clock stops at release, so the frost never returns before it lifts. Ink **0.45** under the frost, **0.9** where the voice has cleared it. As the frost forms or lifts, the strokes as seen through it move straight between muted and that strength, so they never peak on the way.
  **Why:** revised 2026-11-04 (maintainer, on device). The shader, as ported from the study, aimed the ink at its cleared strength until the frost had formed: the strokes brightened under the thin frost, then dimmed as it thickened, a pulse on entry and exit of about 6 L* in Light mode and 10 in Dark. An env-gated snapshot test measures it on rendered frames.
- **Arrival:** the frost forms and the ink rises under it together, over **0.6 s** each (0.40 s as first approved). **Why:** in sequence, the muted strokes dimmed under fresh frost and bounced back, and the arrival pulsed (maintainer, 2026-10-31).
- **Why a steady cadence and three strengths:** a cadence reads the same however fast you speak; with strength following the level, a raised voice gave only 0.4–0.6 per ripple, and refraction alone is the least visible cue. Three tiers make soft, normal and raised visibly different.
- **The shader's interface** is `GlassRenderer.arguments`: every value the shader takes, named and in its order, with the tuning from `Constants` and the veil, highlight and shade colors from `Palette.HUD`; a test holds it to the shader's own signature. The veil and the light are overlays on the strokes, so the panel's surface shows between them. The shader is `KawaGlass.metal`, a SwiftUI layer effect, compiled to `KawaGlass.metallib` by the Makefile and asserted by `make verify` ([../architecture/distribution.md](../architecture/distribution.md)). If it fails to load, River logs once at `.error` and draws the muted strokes, so the panel is never blank.

## Stream, dropped

Stream was built beside Glass as a second style, the default, chosen with a temporary picker in the menu bar dropdown: a band of square cells across the panel that carried the level left to right through the strokes, brighter inside them. It went through both smoke rounds. On 2026-11-03 the maintainer dropped it, with the picker and its setting, to focus on mastering one concept, Glass. Its study pages stay in [studies/kawa/](studies/kawa/) as the record; the tuning it was built with is in [0035](../planning/0035_kawa-identity-and-indicator-styles.md).

## The level scale

Glass reads one smoothed level (`KawaIndicatorEngine`: rises over about 0.05 s, falls over about 0.25 s, with a ±18 % jitter while you speak). Its input is loudness in dBFS from the floor `markMeterFloorDecibels` (the capture trim's floor, about −50) to a ceiling of **−16** (`markMeterCeilingDecibels`), on a curve of exponent **1.5** (`markMeterCurve`). Below the floor it reads silence, so the indicator never moves for a recording the trim then discards. Each level reading (about 14 a second) carries the loudest buffer, about 10 ms, since the last one (peak hold), and the level clips at −12 dBFS (`inputLevelReferenceRMS` 0.25), above the ceiling.

**Why:** an on-device trace (built-in microphone, 2026-11-02) put a quiet room at about −62 dBFS, soft speech to about −39, normal speech about −33, and raised speech about −22 with peaks near −15. At the first calibration (ceiling −28, no curve, the last buffer sampled, the level clipping at −22) soft speech filled half the scale and normal speech nearly all of it, and an air-conditioning hum read as speech. Glass's tier boundaries (0.32, 0.60) and threshold (0.164) are unchanged in level units; on this scale they sit near −34, −26 and −40 dBFS, and separate soft, normal and raised speech ("excellent", maintainer, 2026-11-03).

## Preparing, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency

- **Preparing the model:** the muted strokes held still at 20 %, and the timeline stops redrawing; the label above speaks. The full preparing and downloading treatment is deferred.
- **Reduce Motion:** every envelope becomes a 0.2 s linear fade with no rise, and the level has no jitter. Glass has no ripples, and the level clears the frost directly, held at its value from the release, so the frost never fills back in while it lifts.
- **Reduce Transparency:** nothing changes. The panel's surface is opaque in every case since 2026-11-04 ([color.md](color.md)).

## Color: monochrome

The panel draws the mark and Glass in the HUD's text color (`ink` on the dark surface, `charcoal` on the light one), at three strengths: muted (20 %), Glass's ink under the frost (45 %) and where it is cleared (90 %). No accent appears anywhere in the panel; it stays the controls' tint ([color.md](color.md)).

## Deferred and open

- **Deferred:** the full preparing and downloading treatment; a model-selection onboarding step.
- **Done: Glass contrast and the panel's surface** (asked 2026-11-03, settled on the Glass study 2026-11-03 and 2026-11-04). Smoke round 2 found the ripples hard to see at a normal voice and frosted and cleared too alike; the retune above answers it with per-tier windows and stronger rings. Every floating surface became an opaque sheet lit from the upper left ([color.md](color.md)).
- **Open:** Glass's frosted ink (45 %) measures 2.65:1 on the light tint before the frost's blur and veil, below 3:1 ([color.md](color.md)).
- **Open:** whether the 20 % muted strokes need a lift under Increase Contrast.

## The study record

[studies/kawa/](studies/kawa/) holds the shipped generator chain and pages (run with `node`; earlier rounds stay out of the repo):

- **Shape:** `kawa-concepts-r1.mjs` and `kawa-concepts-r3.mjs` (the strokes as brush movements), `kawa-brush.mjs` and `kawa-fit.mjs`, producing `kawa-paths-r3.json`.
- **Centring:** `kawa-centering.mjs` → `kawa-centering-r1.json`, and the page `kawa-centering-r1.html`. **At rest:** `kawa-static-r1.html` (icon, menu bar, panel).
- **Motion:** `kawa-motion.mjs` → `kawa-motion-r1.json`; `build-studies.mjs` builds `kawa-studies-r1.html` from `kawa-studies.template-r1.html`. That page opens on the approved values; `kawa-motion-studies-v10.html` is the page as published, on its original settings.
- **Glass and the surface:** `build-glass.mjs` and `kawa-glass-r1.html` (Glass's contrast and the lit panel surface) stay local, ignored by git; their chosen values are in `Constants`.
- **To Swift:** `kawa-swift.mjs` → `Sources/River/Views/KawaMark+Outlines.swift`.
